01 / MONITOR

Live charger status

02 / MEASURE

Energy intelligence

Connected infrastructure

Designed to integrate.
Built to evolve.

We design an integration path around your charger capabilities and existing infrastructure. OCPP 1.6J or 2.0.1, MQTT, HTTP APIs and Modbus gateways may be applicable depending on the hardware.

Protocol compatibility, remote operations and device diagnostics must be confirmed against each charger model. The OCPP layer and ThingsBoard connectivity are subject to technical validation.

From charger to insight

EV charger → compatible OCPP server / gateway → ThingsBoard telemetry and rules → customer dashboards and alerts.
