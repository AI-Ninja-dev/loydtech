- Milesight: official header SVG (`symbol-milesight-colorful`) from https://www.milesight.com/ . ViewBox tightened around the existing artwork, with padding; paths unchanged.

- MOKO SMART: original logo image and transparency mask extracted from page 8 of https://www.mokoblue.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Panic-Button-Product-Brief_V1.0_20230619.pdf . Brand reference: https://www.mokosmart.com/media-kit/ .

The carousel preserves its existing five-company lineup, 34-second loop, hover pause, edge fade and reduced-motion fallback. Keyboard focus now also pauses it. Both copies come from one data list; the duplicate is hidden from assistive technology. Neutral white logo cards keep the official artwork legible independently of the surrounding light or dark background. Fixed card geometry and contained images prevent distortion and layout shifts.
